Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

DailyExpenseAIPro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information. All data is stored locally on your device.

2. Local Data Storage

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Financial transactions (amount, date, category, notes)
  • Account balances and names
  • Budget settings and goals
  • App preferences and settings

All data is stored using Apple's Core Data framework with encryption when enabled.

3. Cloud Sync (Premium Feature)

Cloud sync is an optional premium feature that uses iCloud to synchronize data across your devices. When enabled:

  • Data is encrypted before being uploaded to iCloud
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data
  • iCloud terms of service and privacy policy apply

4. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions for enhanced functionality:

  • Camera: For taking photos of receipts (optional)
  • Face ID/Touch ID: For app security (optional)
  • Location: To tag transaction locations (optional)
  • Microphone: For voice input (optional)

All permissions are optional. The app works without any of them.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained only on your device. You can export or delete your data at any time from the app settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at the support page within the app.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored locally
  • Export your data in CSV or PDF format
  • Delete all your data from the app
  • Disable optional features and permissions
