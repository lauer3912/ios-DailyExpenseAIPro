Daily Expense AI Pro uses on-device AI to analyze your spending habits and provide personalized insights. Unlike other expense trackers that just show numbers, we help you understand WHERE your money goes and HOW to save more.

- Create monthly budgets for any category

**Powerful Analytics**
- See your spending patterns with beautiful charts

**Recurring Transactions**
- Automate regular bills and subscriptions
- Never forget a payment again

- Your financial data stays private
