Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

Daily Expense AI Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Local Data Storage: All financial data you enter (transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, goals) is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or transmit your financial data to any external servers.

No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics services. We have no trackers, cookies, or data collection mechanisms from external companies.

AI Technology

On-Device AI: Daily Expense AI Pro uses on-device machine learning for the following features:

  • Spending predictions and insights
  • Smart category suggestions
  • Duplicate transaction detection

All AI processing occurs locally on your device. No personal or financial data is sent to cloud-based AI services.

In-App Purchases

Subscription payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled exclusively by Apple.

Data Security

Your data remains on your device under your control. You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.
