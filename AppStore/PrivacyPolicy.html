Daily Expense AI Pro

Privacy Policy · Last Updated: May 20, 2026

In short: Your financial data stays on your device. We don't collect, track, or sell any personal information. Our AI runs entirely on your device.

1. What We Collect

Daily Expense AI Pro is designed around privacy. All your data is processed and stored locally on your device. Here's what we collect:

  • Financial transactions -- Expenses, income, categories, accounts, budgets, and goals you enter -- stored locally on your device only
  • App preferences -- Settings and customization choices you make within the app, stored securely on your device
  • Photos -- Receipt photos you attach to transactions -- stored locally and never transmitted anywhere

2. What We Don't Collect

We explicitly commit to never collecting or accessing:

  • Your personal identity information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Your precise location data
  • Your contacts, calendar, or messages
  • Your browsing history or web access records
  • Any data that can personally identify you
  • Any data from third-party apps

3. AI Technology

On-Device AI: Daily Expense AI Pro uses on-device machine learning for the following features:

  • Spending predictions and insights
  • Smart category suggestions
  • Duplicate transaction detection

All AI processing occurs entirely on your device. No personal or financial data is ever sent to cloud-based AI services.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on your device using iOS's built-in storage (UserDefaults and local database). We do not maintain any external servers that hold your personal data.

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Daily Expense AI Pro uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store -- For in-app purchases and subscription management (governed by Apple's privacy policy)
  • Apple Store Connect -- For app distribution and updates

We do not share any data with advertising networks, analytics companies, or data brokers.

6. In-App Purchases

Subscription payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled exclusively by Apple.

7. Children's Privacy

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your financial data remains on your device under your control.
